# Play Store listing: Bhagavatham Wallpapers

**App name** (30 max): Bhagavatham Wallpapers

**Short description** (80 max):
A Bhagavatam painting and its story as your wallpaper, a new one every day.

**Full description:**

Wake up to the Srimad Bhagavatam. Every day your phone wallpaper changes to the next incident: a classical painting in a gold frame, with the skandha, title and a short story of what happened.

HOW IT WORKS
• One incident a day, in order: 318 incidents through all twelve skandhas, from the sages of Naimisharanya and King Parikshit to Krishna's childhood in Vrindavana, his life in Mathura and Dwaraka, and Markandeya's vision. Day one is the day you install it.
• The wallpaper changes by itself after midnight and keeps working after your phone restarts.
• Put it on the home screen, the lock screen or both.
• Swipe left or right in the app to read ahead or look back, and set any incident as your wallpaper.
• Hide the story text if you only want the painting.

SIX LANGUAGES
English, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Hindi. The app follows your phone's language, or you can choose one in Settings.

THE PAINTINGS
Bhagavata Purana folios by Pahari, Kangra, Guler and Rajasthani masters such as Manaku of Guler, Mughal-era paintings, Raja Ravi Varma and more, all public domain or freely licensed from Wikimedia Commons. Every painting is credited with its artist, licence and a link to its source.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
All paintings are inside the app, so it works without internet. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Bhagavatham is the second app in the series, after Ramayanam Wallpapers. Mahabharatham is coming next.
